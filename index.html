How wholesaling works

1. Find a distressed deal — probate, foreclosure, vacant, tax-delinquent. The seller needs out; the spread is your room.

2. Get it under contract at the low price, with an assignment clause.

3. Flip the contract to a cash buyer for more than your contract price.

4. Keep the difference — the assignment fee — at closing. You never owned the house.

Honest notes
